## Approaches on the Table

**A. Story-first (recommended)**
Lead with one polished, playable story on itch.io and IFDB. Players discover the open-source engine after engaging with the story. Leverages the live web player (click-to-play, no install). Developer/author angle is secondary in descriptions and README.

**B. Author-first**
Lead with the engine on Show HN and intfiction.org tools section. Story library is proof-of-concept. Higher ceiling but more volatile.

**C. Simultaneous multi-front**
Tailored posts to all three communities on the same day. Needs three strong assets ready at once: compelling story, clean README, "build with this" pitch.
